Welcome to StyBay.

At StyBay, we believe discovering fashion should be personal, intuitive, and secure. Protecting your privacy is an important part of creating an experience you can trust.

This Privacy Policy explains how StyBay ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit our website, join our early-access waitlist, or use our fashion discovery platform and related services as they become available.

Our website is available at https://stybay.com.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with StyBay.

Information You Provide

When you join our waitlist, contact us, or use available services, we may collect:

- Contact Information: Your email address, name, or other contact details you voluntarily provide.
- Account Information: Registration details and profile preferences if account features become available.
- Communications: Questions, feedback, messages, or support requests you send us.

Fashion Preferences and Discovery Activity

When our fashion discovery features become available, we may collect and process information such as:

- Fashion searches and style preferences.
- Preferred colors, aesthetics, brands, and categories.
- Products viewed, explored, or saved.
- Wishlist items and personal collections.
- Interactions with fashion recommendations.
- Images voluntarily uploaded for visual fashion search, if that feature is introduced.

This information may help us create a more relevant and personalized fashion discovery experience.

Automatically Collected Information

When you interact with our website or platform, certain technical information may be collected through our systems and service providers, including:

- Device type and browser information.
- IP address and approximate location.
- Pages visited and website interactions.
- Access times and referral information.
- Technical logs, error reports, and performance data.
- Cookies and similar technologies, where enabled.

We aim to limit the collection of information to what is necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information to operate, maintain, develop, and improve StyBay.

Providing Our Services

To manage early-access registrations, provide available features, maintain accounts, respond to inquiries, and support users.

Personalized Fashion Discovery

When available, to understand fashion preferences, improve search relevance, and provide recommendations that reflect individual interests and styles.

AI-Powered Search and Recommendations

To support intelligent fashion search, interpret style-related descriptions, identify relevant products, and improve recommendation quality.

Improving StyBay

To analyze platform performance, identify technical issues, understand feature usage, and develop a better user experience.

Communication and Updates

To send requested early-access invitations, waitlist updates, important service notices, and other communications you have agreed to receive.

Security and Compliance

To protect the platform, prevent unauthorized activity, investigate potential misuse, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

3. Artificial Intelligence and Personalization

StyBay is developing AI-powered fashion discovery features designed to make finding and exploring fashion more intuitive.

As these features become available, our technology may use information such as search descriptions, selected preferences, browsing interactions, and saved products to provide more relevant fashion discovery experiences.

These capabilities may include:

- Understanding fashion searches written in natural language.
- Identifying products matching particular moods, occasions, or aesthetics.
- Recommending similar fashion products and styles.
- Personalizing discovery feeds based on user preferences.
- Improving the relevance of product search and recommendations.

Some AI features may involve third-party technology providers that process the information necessary to deliver those features.

Before enabling processing involving external AI providers, we will assess the relevant data-handling arrangements and provide information about those providers' roles, retention practices, and any model-training use where required.

AI-generated results are intended to support discovery and may not always be complete or accurate.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal or confidential information into fashion search fields.

Depending on the technologies enabled, these may include:

Analytics Cookies: Help us understand website traffic, performance, and user interactions.

StyBay is designed to help users discover fashion products from different online stores and retailers.

When you select a product and proceed to an external retailer, you may be redirected to that retailer's website or application.

Those external platforms operate independently and may collect information under their own privacy policies.

StyBay does not control the privacy practices, data collection, payment processing, or security measures of third-party retailers.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of external websites before completing purchases or sharing personal information.
